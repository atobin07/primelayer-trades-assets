Second source: Coinbase BTC-USD daily candles (read only) confirm the 30 Jun close 58,523.93 and the 16 Oct close 81,500.04.

Figures used:

Check 1: running peak scan. Check 2: minimum over all days of close divided by highest prior close. Both gave 27 Jan to 30 Mar, down 9.1331 percent.
* BTC 1 Jan to 16 Oct 2026: 289 days, 77 closed 2 percent or more away from the prior close, 10 of them Saturday or Sunday (UTC), 4 up, 6 down. Largest weekend move down 6.49 percent on Sat 31 Jan. Check 1: close over prior close. Check 2: close over same bar open (Coinbase opens equal prior closes). Both gave 77 and 10.
* BTC from the 30 Jun low close 58,523.93 to the 16 Oct close 81,500.04: up 39.26 percent, shown as 39.3, over 108 days. Confirmed against Coinbase candles.

## Calculation code

```python
import json,datetime as dt
s=json.load(open('data/spy.json'));b=json.load(open('data/btc.json'))
sd=[dt.datetime.fromtimestamp(t,dt.UTC).date() for t in s['t']]
bd=[dt.date(2025,12,1)+dt.timedelta(days=i) for i in range(len(b['c']))]
print("=== A. SPY 2026 largest close-to-close drawdown, Dec 31 2025 through Oct 16 2026")
i0=sd.index(dt.date(2025,12,31)); C=s['c'][i0:]; D=sd[i0:]
peak=C[0];pk=0;best=(0,0,0)
for k,c in enumerate(C):
    if c>peak: peak=c;pk=k
    dd=c/peak-1
    if dd<best[0]: best=(dd,pk,k)
dd,pk,tr=best
rec=next(k for k in range(tr,len(C)) if C[k]>=C[pk])
print('peak',D[pk],C[pk],'trough',D[tr],C[tr],'dd%',dd*100,'sessions peak->trough',tr-pk,'recovered',D[rec],C[rec],'sessions trough->recovery',rec-tr,'peak->recovery',rec-pk)
print('SPY Oct16 close',C[-1],D[-1],'ytd%',(C[-1]/C[0]-1)*100, 'max close',max(C),D[C.index(max(C))])
# method 2: brute force all pairs
m=min(((C[j]/max(C[:j+1])-1),j) for j in range(len(C)))
j=m[1];p=max(range(j+1),key=lambda q:C[q])
print('check2 dd%',m[0]*100,D[p],D[j])
print("=== B. BTC days moving 2%+ by weekday, Jan 1 through Oct 16 2026 (UTC daily closes)")
j0=bd.index(dt.date(2025,12,31)); j1=bd.index(dt.date(2026,10,16))
days=[(bd[k],b['c'][k]/b['c'][k-1]-1) for k in range(j0+1,j1+1)]
big=[(d,r) for d,r in days if abs(r)>=0.02]
we=[(d,r) for d,r in big if d.weekday()>=5]
print('days',len(days),'2pct days',len(big),'weekend 2pct',len(we),'weekend days total',sum(d.weekday()>=5 for d,_ in days))
print('weekend big list',[(str(d),round(r*100,2)) for d,r in we])
# method 2: close vs open per bar (open==prev close on Coinbase)
big2=[k for k in range(j0+1,j1+1) if abs(b['c'][k]/b['o'][k]-1)>=0.02]
print('check2 using open->close',len(big2), sum(bd[k].weekday()>=5 for k in big2))
print("=== C. BTC from Jun 30 low close to Oct 16 close")
lo=bd.index(dt.date(2026,6,30))
print('Jun30',b['c'][lo],'Oct16',b['c'][j1],'chg%',(b['c'][j1]/b['c'][lo]-1)*100,'days',j1-lo)
# biggest 4-day gain in period
best=max(((b['c'][k+4]/b['c'][k]-1),k) for k in range(lo,j1-3))
print('best 4 day', best[0]*100, bd[best[1]], '->', bd[best[1]+4], b['c'][best[1]], b['c'][best[1]+4])
for k in range(best[1]-1,best[1]+6): print(bd[k],b['c'][k],round((b['c'][k]/b['c'][k-1]-1)*100,2))
print("=== D. week Oct 12-16 SPY, BTC Oct 10-16")
for k in range(len(sd)-6,len(sd)): print(sd[k],s['o'][k],s['c'][k],round((s['c'][k]/s['c'][k-1]-1)*100,2))
```
